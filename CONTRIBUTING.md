# Contributing to Void

First off, thank you for considering contributing to Void! It's people like you that make Void such a great platform for bringing ideas to life.

## Ways to Contribute

There are many ways you can contribute to Void:

- **Submit Ideas:** Have a great concept? Share it!
- **Discuss:** Help refine ideas by participating in GitHub Discussions and Issue threads.
- **Implement:** Write code to bring approved ideas to reality.
- **Review:** Help review Pull Requests to ensure quality and safety.

---

## 💡 How to Submit an Idea

1. Navigate to the **Issues** tab.
2. Click on **New Issue**.
3. Select the **Idea Submission** template.
4. Fill out the template completely:
   - Provide a clear and descriptive title.
   - Explain the problem your idea solves.
   - Describe the proposed solution.
   - List any potential challenges.
5. Click **Submit new issue**.

---

## 🛠️ How to Claim an Idea

Want to build an idea? Here's how to claim it:

1. Browse the **Issues** tab for ideas labeled `Approved`.
2. Find an idea you want to work on.
3. Leave a comment on the issue stating: *"I would like to work on this!"*
4. A maintainer will assign the issue to you and change the label to `In Progress`.
5. You can now start building!

---

## 💻 How to Submit a PR (Pull Request)

1. **Fork** the repository to your own GitHub account.
2. **Clone** the repository to your local machine.
3. **Create a branch** for your feature (`git checkout -b feature/idea-name`).
4. **Implement** your changes.
5. **Test** your changes locally to ensure they work as expected.
6. **Commit** your changes with clear, descriptive commit messages.
7. **Push** your branch to your forked repository.
8. Open a **Pull Request (PR)** against the `main` branch of the Void repository.
9. Link the PR to the original Issue you claimed.

---

## 🎨 Code Style Guidelines

While the specifics may vary depending on the language used for a particular idea, we ask that you follow these general best practices:

- Write clean, readable, and well-documented code.
- Follow the standard style guides for the language you are using.
- Keep your commits atomic and logically separated.
- Ensure your code does not introduce new security vulnerabilities.

---

## 🔍 Review Process

All Pull Requests require review before they can be merged.

1. A maintainer or community member will review your code.
2. They may request changes or ask clarifying questions.
3. Please be responsive to feedback and update your PR accordingly.
4. Once approved, a maintainer will merge your PR.

---

## 🤝 Community Guidelines

We expect all contributors to adhere to our community guidelines to ensure a welcoming and inclusive environment. Please read our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before contributing.

---

## 🆘 Getting Help

If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to:

- Ask in the relevant **Issue** or **Discussion** thread.
- Open a new Discussion in the **Q&A** category.
